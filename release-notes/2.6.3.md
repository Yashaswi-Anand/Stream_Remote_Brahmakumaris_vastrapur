Desktop app for the stream PC (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).

**Download:** `Zoom-Stream-Automation-Setup-2.6.3.exe`. Installs a new PC, or upgrades the installed app; settings and pairing are kept.

**New in 2.6.3**
- The app's tabs (Home, Auto Power On, Remote, Update) have icons, matching the website's menu.
- Website on phones: the menu (Home, Download, Admin, Sign out) is behind a ☰ button, so the header takes less space.

**Since 2.6.2:** rename this PC from the Remote tab.

Upgrade: open the app → **Update** → **Download & install**.
