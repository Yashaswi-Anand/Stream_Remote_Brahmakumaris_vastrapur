Desktop app for the stream PC (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).

**Download:** `Zoom-Stream-Automation-Setup-2.6.2.exe`. Installs a new PC, or upgrades the installed app; settings and pairing are kept.

**New in 2.6.2**
- Rename this PC from the desktop app: **Remote** tab → **Rename** next to "This PC is called". The new name shows on the website's system list at once.
- The owner can still rename any PC on the website (✎ next to the system dropdown, or Admin → PCs → Rename).
- Clearer "Add a PC" box on the website: steps, Copy buttons, countdown and a "Paired" notice.

Upgrade: open the app → **Update** → **Download & install**.
