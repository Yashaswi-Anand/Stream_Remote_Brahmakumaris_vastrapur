Desktop app for the stream PC (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).

**Download:** `Zoom-Stream-Automation-Setup-2.5.1.exe`. Installs a new PC, or upgrades the installed app; settings and pairing are kept.

**New in 2.5.1: fixes for Zoom steps when browser windows are open**
- The Zoom meeting window is now reliably brought to the front (also when YouTube Studio or Zoom's YouTube page was just opened in Chrome), so the "More", "Share" and "End" buttons are found.
- Zoom's share dialog is scrolled when the "ZSA Stream Image" window is further down the list.
- The meeting is brought back to the front after the YouTube steps, before sharing the image.

Upgrade: open the app → **Update** → **Download & install**.
