Desktop app for the stream PC (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).

**Download:** `Zoom-Stream-Automation-Setup-2.6.1.exe`. Installs a new PC, or upgrades the installed app; settings and pairing are kept.

**New in 2.6.1**
- Rename a system: the owner taps ✎ next to the system dropdown on the website (or Admin → PCs → Rename). The new name shows in the dropdown at once and in the desktop app's Remote tab.

**Since 2.6.0:** the website is called Stream Automation; with two or more paired systems the header has a dropdown, and buttons act only on the selected system.

Upgrade: open the app → **Update** → **Download & install**.
