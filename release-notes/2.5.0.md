Desktop app for the stream PC (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).

**Download:** `Zoom-Stream-Automation-Setup-2.5.0.exe`. Installs a new PC, or upgrades the installed app; settings, meeting ID, images, schedule and the Stream Remote pairing are kept.

**New in 2.5.0**
- Start Stream checklist: every step has a checkbox (all ticked by default); unticked steps are skipped. Also on the Stream Remote website and used by the daily schedule.
- Music: play songs from a folder (one after another, random order) or a YouTube link (on a loop), with Play / Stop in the app and on the website.
- Start Stream plays the music as its last step; End Stream stops it. It is heard in Zoom when sharing with sound.

Upgrade from 2.2.0 or later: open the app → **Update** → **Download & install**.

Website: https://stream-remote-bkvastrapur.web.app · Not code-signed: if Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.
