Desktop app for the stream PC (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).

**Download:** `Zoom-Stream-Automation-Setup-2.4.1.exe`. Installs a new PC, or upgrades the installed app; settings, meeting ID, images, schedule and the Stream Remote pairing are kept.

**New in 2.4.1**
- YouTube opens in the Chrome profile already signed in to the channel's account (no sign-in or 2-step code); Zoom's YouTube page opens in the same profile.
- Always selects today's scheduled stream (via Manage if needed) and clicks Go live; another day is never selected.
- Runs in the background (system tray) and starts at Windows sign-in, so the Stream Remote website can always reach the PC.

Upgrade from 2.2.0 or later: open the app → **Update** → **Download & install**.

Website: https://stream-remote-bkvastrapur.web.app · Not code-signed: if Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.
