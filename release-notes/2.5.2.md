Desktop app for the stream PC (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).

**Download:** `Zoom-Stream-Automation-Setup-2.5.2.exe`. Installs a new PC, or upgrades the installed app; settings and pairing are kept.

**New in 2.5.2**
- Works with Zoom's new More menu: the **Livestream** tile that starts YouTube directly (no "Live on YouTube" submenu) is now recognised, so "Live on YouTube is not in Zoom's More menu" no longer appears.
- Recognises Zoom's new "Stop livestream" wording when ending the stream.

**Since 2.5.1:** the Zoom meeting window is reliably brought to the front when browser windows are open, and the share dialog scrolls to find the image window.

Upgrade: open the app → **Update** → **Download & install**.
